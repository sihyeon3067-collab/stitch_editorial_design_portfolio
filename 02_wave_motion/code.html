Wave

PROJECT TYPE 3D Motion / Typography
YEAR 2024

ABOUT

An exploration of physical dynamics translated into digital typography. The Wave Motion project captures the tension between rigid grids and fluid momentum. By simulating liquid physics on typography arrays, we created a rhythmic visual system that constantly redefines its boundaries.

RHYTHM &
COLLISION

The core visual thesis relies on high-speed collision mechanics slowed to a hypnotic pace. The resulting forms dictate the layout, breaking standard typographic rules while maintaining a structured chaos.
