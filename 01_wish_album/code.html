About

The "Wish Album" visual identity explores the intersection of nostalgic artifacts and contemporary luxury. By reimagining the physical music format as a high-end collectible, the design language utilizes stark contrasts, minimal typography, and a signature primary pink hue to elevate the tactile experience of listening.
